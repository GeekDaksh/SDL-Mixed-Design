Pramāṇa (प्रमाण) means proof, measure and standard. It is an AI lab assistant for concrete made with manufactured sand, trained on 401 mix records from ten Maharashtra plants. It designs mixes to IS 456 and IS 10262, throws out every mix that breaks a rule, and picks the next trial for the lab. Each dot is one candidate mix.

Material values are the project's defaults: crushed sand (Zone II, specific gravity 2.65), 20 mm angular basalt (2.74), fly ash (2.20), PCE dose 0.62% to 1.48% of binder, and the paper's fitted water cut, r = 3.709 + 14.037 × dose. Cost and carbon use placeholder rates, not quotes. Wheelbarrow loads assume about 100 kg each. The IS code PDFs are checked against their hashes on the author's machine; they are copyrighted, so they aren't shipped here.

Can it hold the slump?

The order asks for 120 mm slump, give or take 25 mm. The model can hit 120 mm on average. The real question is whether it can promise every batch lands inside the window. Move the sliders.

Can't promise it

At the order's own settings (±25 mm, 90%) the system answers "not achievable", and it says so before anyone casts a cube. The spread comes from testing on a mix family the model never saw: 19.4 mm standard deviation, with a further 10.6 mm shift in the centre.

Are the odds honest?

Before a trial, the system says how likely each mix is to pass. Here is how those odds held up on 71 real strength recipes and 23 slump recipes it had not seen.

Which trial next?

Lab trials cost days. We replayed 12 past orders and compared ways of choosing the next mix. Two things matter: how much of the best cost-and-carbon trade-off you find in 4 trials, and how many of those trials fail.

StrategyBest trade-off found by trial 4Failed trials by trial 4

The chosen strategy (safe-first) gives up some of the trade-off early to cut failed trials from about 2.4 to 0.7. Random choice is the average of 300 runs.

Does it work at a new plant?

We trained on four plants and tested on the one left out, each in turn. Strength predictions travel well between plants. Slump predictions don't: the spread is about four times larger.

Tested on recipes it never saw

The lab loop

The system doesn't replace the lab. It decides what the lab should cast next, then learns from the result.

Design

Build the IS 10262 mix for the order, with every value tied to its clause.

Screen

Try every legal combination and drop any mix that breaks IS 456 or sits outside the data.

Trial

Send 5 ranked designs and 3 IS trial mixes to the lab, with batch weights and an Excel pack.

Learn

Log the cube and slump results, refit both models, and plan the next round.
